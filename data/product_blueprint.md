# The AI Creator Workflow System
## Product Blueprint v1.0

### Product Overview
- **Name:** The AI Creator Workflow System
- **Target:** AI content creators, social media creators using AI tools
- **Price:** $27 (launch) → $47 (post-validation)
- **Platform:** Gumroad
- **Format:** PDF + Notion template + video links

**Audience Growth (5 prompts)**
6. Bio Optimizer — platform-specific bio that converts visitors to followers

8. Hashtag Strategy Generator — niche-specific hashtag sets with reach estimates
9. Collaboration Pitch Writer — DM/email templates for brand deals
10. Analytics Interpreter — paste your stats, get actionable next steps

**Monetization (5 prompts)**
11. Product Description Writer — for digital products, courses, services
12. Sales Page Framework — full sales page from product details
13. Email Sequence Builder — 5-email welcome/sales sequence
14. Pricing Strategy Advisor — analyzes your niche and suggests pricing
15. Affiliate Outreach Template — pitch brands for partnership

**Automation (5 prompts)**
16. Workflow Mapper — designs your content workflow from creation to posting
17. Repurposing Engine — one piece of content → 10 platform-specific formats
18. Research Assistant — deep-dive any topic with structured output
19. Competitor Analyzer — analyze top 5 creators in your niche
20. SOP Builder — turn any repeated task into a standard operating procedure
