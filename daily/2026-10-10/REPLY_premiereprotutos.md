## The problem in one line

Omar replied: *"I don't currently have a library of presets, transitions, or plugins ready to
share. If your team can create the entire product from scratch, including the assets,
packaging, and Whop storefront, I'm happy to consider moving forward with the 60/40 revenue
split."*

The Raw-to-Whop model is built on the creator supplying raw files in ~15 minutes. With no
asset library, the operator would be creating **100% of the product IP for 40% of the
revenue**, while the creator contributes only distribution. That is the `@war_forum` failure
the kit already recorded: a full product built, creator withdrew, operator left with the work.

A 60/40 split in the creator's favour is only defensible when the creator is contributing the
IP. This is not a fit at 60/40, and saying so plainly is the honest move — the kit's quality
bar is *"if a lead is weak, say drop it and why."*
