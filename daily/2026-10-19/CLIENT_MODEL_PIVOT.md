## The Pivot

The operator's exact words: *"All are Rev share. I need you to prepare one I can do without a Rev share & get clients."*

The rev-share model is not abandoned — it's being supplemented. The client model sells the same core competency (packaging, storefront setup, 14-day launch sequences) as a fixed-fee service instead of a revenue-share partnership.

---

## Why This Niche

**Target:** AI-video and creative-production studios/agencies that already sell services to clients but have no digital-product backend.

**Why these prospects:**
1. They already have paying clients (proven demand)
2. They already create digital assets (project files, presets, templates, workflows)
3. They have no storefront, no productized offer, no subscription tier
4. The gap is packaging + launch — exactly what the operation has built three times
5. They're studios/agencies, not solo creators — they can pay $1,500–$3,500 without blinking

**Why NOT solo creators (for this model):**
- Solo creators at 10K-100K can't afford $1,500+ upfront
- Rev-share is the right model for creators with audiences but no products
- The client model targets people who already have revenue and want to add a product line

---

## Scope (NARROWED)

**What the operator CAN deliver:**
- Product packaging (PDF guides, preset packs, workflow vaults)
- Whop storefront setup (listing, checkout, split-pay, legal pack)
- 14-day Instagram Stories launch sequence (operator writes every caption, client posts)
- Demand test (3-story carousel, measurement, go/no-go)

**What the operator CANNOT deliver (confirmed by operator):**
- Visual production (video editing, motion graphics, VFX compositing)
- Original asset creation (transitions, LUTs, sound effects — these must come from the client)
- Client-facing work on a paying client's timeline

**The offer is packaging only.** The client supplies the raw assets. The operator packages them into a sellable product, sets up the storefront, and runs the launch.

## Pricing

| Tier | Price | What's Included |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| **Pilot** | $400–$750 | One product packaged + Whop storefront setup + demand test. Fixed fee. |
| **Build** | $1,500–$3,500 | Full product build + storefront + legal pack + 14-day launch sequence. Fixed fee. |
| **Launch Retainer** | $750–$1,500/mo | Ongoing: new products monthly, launch management, storefront optimization, reporting. |

**Why these prices:**
- Pilot: low enough to say yes without a committee. High enough to filter out tyre-kickers.
- Build: comparable to what a freelance designer charges for a brand identity package. Studios spend this on a single project.
- Retainer: cheaper than hiring a junior marketer. More valuable because the operator has built this three times.

**Payment:** Direct invoice. No Whop (sidesteps the unverified Whop→Kenya payout path). Bank transfer, Wise, or Payoneer.

## The 14-Day Target

**Goal:** ONE PAID PILOT in 14 days. Not one signed partner. Not one launch. One person who pays $400–$750 for a packaged product + storefront.

**Why a pilot first:**

- The case study is the sales tool for the Build and Retainer tiers
- Without a pilot, every other tier is a hypothetical
