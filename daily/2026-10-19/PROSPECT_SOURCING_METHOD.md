# 30-PROSPECT SOURCING METHOD
**Target:** AI-video and creative-production studios/agencies with no digital-product backend
**Date:** 2026-10-19

---

## Method

The sandbox cannot reach Instagram or scrape live data. The operator (or a VA) must run the sourcing. Here's the exact process:

### Step 1: Instagram Search (manual, 30-60 min)

Search these terms on Instagram:
- "[city] video production studio"
- "[city] motion graphics agency"
- "[city] VFX studio"
- "AI video studio"
- "creative production agency"
- "video editing studio"
- "3D animation studio"
- "filmmaking studio"

For each result, check:
- [ ] Has a website linked in bio
- [ ] Website shows client work (portfolio, case studies)
- [ ] No digital product storefront (no Whop, Gumroad, Shopify product page)
- [ ] Has an Instagram following (any size)
- [ ] Posts regularly (at least 1x/week)
- [ ] Bio email or "Contact" link available

### Step 2: LinkedIn Search (manual, 30-60 min)

Search LinkedIn for:
- "Creative Director" + "Video Production"
- "Founder" + "Motion Graphics Studio"
- "CEO" + "VFX Agency"
- "Head of Production" + "[city]"

For each result, check:
- [ ] Company page exists with 2-20 employees
- [ ] Company posts creative work (not just corporate updates)
- [ ] No digital product listed on company website
- [ ] Founder/CD is active on LinkedIn (posts, comments)

### Step 3: Agency Directories (manual, 30-60 min)

Browse these directories:
- **Clutch.co** — filter by "Video Production", "Motion Graphics", "VFX"
- **DesignRush** — filter by "Video Production Agency"
- **The Manifest** — filter by "Video Production Companies"
- **Dribbble** — search for studios/agencies (not solo freelancers)

For each result, check the same criteria as Step 1.

### Step 4: Instagram Explore (passive, ongoing)

Follow 10-15 production studios. Instagram's "Suggested accounts" will surface similar pages over 3-5 days. Check each suggested account against the criteria.

## Prioritization

Tier the 30 prospects:

| Tier | Criteria | Outreach first? |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| **A** | 5K-50K followers, active posting, clear product gap, bio email available | Yes — send first |
| **B** | 50K-200K followers, or no bio email (use LinkedIn/website contact) | Yes — send second |
| **C** | 200K+ followers, or unclear product gap | Maybe — only if A and B don't convert |
