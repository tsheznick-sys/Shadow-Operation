## How It Works

1. **You send me your raw assets** — project files, presets, templates, whatever you already have.
2. **I package them into a sellable product** — structured, designed, listed on Whop.
3. **You post the launch content** — I write every caption, every frame, every CTA. You post it in your voice.
4. **Revenue hits your account** — Whop handles payments. You get paid.
