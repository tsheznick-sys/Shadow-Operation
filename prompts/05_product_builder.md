# 05 PRODUCT BUILDER (only after G1 + G2 + G3 pass)
CHOOSE the product from comment evidence, not taste: what do people ASK for ("how do I", "where can I get")? Skill-transfer products (prompts, templates, workflows, step-by-step systems) beat "inspiration" products.
SPEC (write before building): buyer, painful problem, promised outcome, the 24-hour quick win, contents map, price, format (PDF/EPUB + optional Notion/Drive pack), what is NOT included, support policy, refund policy.
STRUCTURE: Quick-start win (first 15 min) -> 5-9 modules -> copy/paste assets (prompts, templates, checklists) -> common mistakes -> 30-day plan -> bonus. Every module ends with an action.
QUALITY BAR: a buyer could get the outcome without the creator present. Would a buyer pay twice? If not, rebuild. Tested instructions only (run every prompt/workflow once and log results).
DESIGN: Black Edition (full-bleed dark, gold/cream, running headers/footers, gold cards). 8-14 pt hierarchy, no walls of text.
PRICING: ladder - tripwire $9-29, core $47-97, subscription $9-29/mo (the MRR path). Do not default to $150-300 for micro-audiences; justify any price above $97 with comparable sales proof.
WHOP LISTING: title (outcome-first), 3-sentence promise, who it's for/not for, what's inside, deliverables list, FAQ, refund policy, preview pages, split-pay configured to the agreed split. Test purchase + test payout path before launch.
LEGAL DRAFTS (lawyer review required): ToS, EULA/license, Privacy, Refund policy, AI-generated-content disclosure, earnings disclaimer if any income claims exist (avoid claims). Seller identity + support email present.
CLEARANCE (G3) before shipping: no third-party IP, no real-person likenesses, ownership of every asset documented, platform content rules checked (religion/health/finance claims).
DELIVERABLES: final files, listing copy, test report, release checklist.
