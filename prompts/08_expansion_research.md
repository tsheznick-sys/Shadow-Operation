# 08 EXPANSION & MARKET RESEARCH (/expand)
Run weekly. Output a ranked experiment list, never a vibes essay.
NICHE DEMAND (use ALL methods, score each 0-5): (1) Google Trends + keyword volume for "[niche] + course/template/prompts/guide"; (2) Whop marketplace: count of products, price, visible sales/members; (3) Gumroad/Etsy/Payhip/Stan bestsellers in the niche; (4) YouTube: views on "how to" videos, comments asking for resources; (5) Reddit/Discord/forums: recurring paid-solution requests; (6) Amazon books/Kindle ranks; (7) creator comments asking "where can I get X" (the strongest signal); (8) competitor price ladder. Output: niche scorecard, TAM-ish estimate with ASSUMPTIONS, "buyers pay for skill" flag.
PRODUCT-FIT: can we produce it with no IP risk, is it a skill transfer, repeatable monthly (MRR potential)?
MRR ARCHITECTURE (the real scale lever): per creator, move from one-off launch to Whop membership (monthly drops, templates, community) priced $9-29/mo. Model portfolio: N creators x members x price x split, churn 8-15%/mo ASSUMPTION. Break-even partnerships needed for target MRR.
CHANNEL EXPERIMENTS (each: hypothesis, cost cap, metric, kill rule): (a) warm outreach via comments/story replies; (b) compliant email to public business emails from a warmed separate domain; (c) creators' comment-to-DM funnels (official, permission-based); (d) Whop content-rewards/clipping campaigns (CPM-paid clipper distribution, roughly $1.50-2.00 per 1,000 views on current campaigns) to drive traffic to products you own; (e) owned niche pages (you control the account = 100% margin, no creator risk); (f) Whop affiliate network for outbound products.
ADJACENT EXPANSION: other platforms the same creators use (YouTube/TikTok), non-English markets, B2B niches where buyers pay more, done-for-you service tiers sold to creators who already monetize.
OUTPUT: top 5 experiments ranked by expected net $/hour, with 7-day test design and kill criteria; plus what to STOP doing.
