# 04 DEMAND TEST (gate G2)
Purpose: prove buyers exist BEFORE anything is built. Replies are cheap; money is the real test.
TWO-TIER TEST
Tier 1 (free): 3-slide carousel or 3 Stories. Slide 1 PROBLEM ("Struggling with [X]? thinking of making something"). Slide 2 SOLUTION (product type, outcome, timeframe, 3 benefits). Slide 3 TEST ("Reply INTERESTED / DM me; responders get first access"). Customize every bracket from the audit.
Tier 2 (the one that counts): PRE-SALE. Reply-ers get a pre-order link at a tripwire price ($9-19) on Whop, honest delivery date (<=7 days), full refund if not delivered. Pre-paid orders are the signal.
PRE-REGISTER THRESHOLDS BEFORE POSTING (write them in the tracker; no moving goalposts):
- Unique story views = V. Interest signals = replies + poll yes + DM keyword (unique people) = I.
- GO: I/V >= 1.5% AND I >= 20, OR >= 5 paid pre-orders.
- AMBER: 0.7-1.5%: change the problem framing/product angle ONCE, re-test once.
- RED: < 0.7% or < 10 absolute: HOLD/KILL. Do not "proceed anyway". (Calibration: a prior test returned ~1 reply on ~388 views = 0.26% = RED.)
THESE THRESHOLDS ARE ASSUMPTIONS. After 5 tests, recalibrate from real pre-sale conversion.
NEVER run the test and the build in parallel. NEVER count likes/views as interest.
OUTPUT: test copy, posting instructions (exact order, no links in tier 1), the pre-registered thresholds, a measurement sheet, and the decision memo (GO/AMBER/RED + reasons) 72h after posting.
