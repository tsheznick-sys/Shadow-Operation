# 06 14-DAY INSTAGRAM STORIES LAUNCH (after commitment + demand GO + product ready)
Roles: you = architect (write every frame + caption pack, specify the TYPE of content per day). Creator = executor (posts, in their own voice, same day). Agent never posts. Creator reviews/approves all copy.
BUY-BUTTON MAP (11): 1 Investment, 2 Identification, 3 Community, 4 Authority, 5 Trust, 6 Reciprocity, 7 Social proof, 8 Story, 9 Anticipation, 10 Early-buyer FOMO, 11 Urgency. (Mapping reconstructed from workbooks; confirm numbering against source.)
PHASE 1 WARM-UP (D1-3, no product, no selling)
D1 SURVEY: 5 golden questions about the problem (poll/question stickers). Log every answer. D2 VALIDATION: reflect answers back, "you're not alone", cite real counts. D3 COMMUNITY: show collective pain/aspiration, "stadium effect".
PHASE 2 VALUE (D4-9, six days of free value)
D4 diagnose + authority (4 beats: the opportunity, the mistake, the insight, a quick win). D5 campfire story, trust. D6 teach the framework (reciprocity). D7 real social proof ONLY (no proof = share creator's own results/process). D8 ownership experience + first scarcity seed (true limits only). D9 pre-cart pause: answer EVERY question. Each unanswered Day-9 question is a Day-10 sale lost.
PHASE 3 MONEY (D10-14)
D10 cart opens: reveal, early-buyer FOMO (real purchases only), expect large share of sales in first 24h. D11 results/testimonials/momentum. D12 objection demolition (FAQ, refund, "is it for me"). D13 urgency: the window closes (true deadline). D14 last-hours push, close hard at the stated time.
DELIVERABLES: per-day frame scripts (3-6 frames), caption pack (.txt), DM reply scripts, link-sticker placement map, posting calendar, metrics log (views, replies, link taps, orders).
EXECUTION QA (earlier launches failed on this): before D1 verify the checkout link opens in a logged-out browser; every day send the creator a checklist (today's frames, order, link sticker, time). Missed day -> same-day nudge; 2 missed days -> pause launch and call. Never rewrite the sequence order mid-flight.
BENCHMARKS (assumptions): link-sticker tap 0.5-2% of story viewers (some sources claim 3-5%); log your own and replace.
POST-MORTEM (/postmortem): funnel counts, revenue, split paid, what broke, rule changes.
