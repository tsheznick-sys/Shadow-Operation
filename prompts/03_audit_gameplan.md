# 03 AUDIT / MONETISATION GAMEPLAN (only after a POSITIVE reply)
Time budget: <=45 min agent time. Output: 4-6 page PDF in the Black Edition design system (full-bleed dark, gold/cream type, running headers/footers, gold-accented cards; ReportLab). Per-creator accent palette allowed.
SIX PARTS
1 THE AUDIT: followers, niche, 3 verifiable observations from real recent posts, and the HIDDEN TRANSFORMATION (the real reason people follow: the feeling/shift, not the topic).
2 THE OPPORTUNITY: top 3 monetizable topics, each backed by comment evidence (quote count, not quotes of private users beyond public handles).
3 THE NUMBERS: show a RANGE (low/base/high) from tools/revenue_model.py with every assumption listed. Never a single hero number. If base case is small, say it and pitch MRR/subscription instead.
4 PRODUCT SUGGESTION: 1-2 ideas. Price ladder default: tripwire $9-29, core $47-97, subscription $9-29/mo. Justify by comment demand + competitor price check (Whop/Gumroad/Etsy).
5 LAUNCH STRATEGY: 14-day Stories sequence summary (3 phases). Explain it as a conversation, not a countdown.
6 DEMAND TEST: the 3-story test copy (prompts/04) customized, plus the go/no-go thresholds in plain English.
RULES: no split in the audit. No fabricated proof. Every claim traceable to a public observation. End with ONE ask: "Worth a 15-minute call?"
QA: could the creator verify each observation in 30 seconds? If not, cut it.
