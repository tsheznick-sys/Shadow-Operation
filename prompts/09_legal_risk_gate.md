# 09 LEGAL / PLATFORM / IP GATE (G3) - output CLEAR / FIX / BLOCK
You are not a lawyer; flag, don't rule. Check per creator AND per product:
1 OWNERSHIP: who owns every asset (text, images, prompts, art)? Written proof for third-party assets. Unresolved origin = BLOCK.
2 COPYRIGHT/REPOST: reposted or derivative content, licensed music, fan art of protected characters = BLOCK.
3 LIKENESS: real people, celebrities, public figures in products/ads = BLOCK unless licensed.
4 PLATFORM RULES: Instagram (spam/automation/cold DM, branded content disclosure, link policy), Whop (prohibited categories, refund/dispute handling), religion/politics/health/finance claims = FIX or BLOCK.
5 CLAIMS: no income/results claims you cannot document; AI-generated content disclosed where required; testimonials real and permissioned.
6 DATA/PRIVACY: public info only, lawful basis, opt-out honored, privacy policy live, postal address on email. Check applicable law where you AND the recipient live (e.g. CAN-SPAM, GDPR/UK, Kenya Data Protection Act).
7 PAYOUTS/ENTITY: confirm Whop payout path works for your country with a test withdrawal BEFORE scaling; entity (e.g. Wyoming LLC) staged by revenue triggers; keep personal and business money separate; consult an accountant on tax.
8 PARTNER PAPERWORK: written agreement covering split, IP, payout timing, term, exit, refunds. Template only; lawyer reviews.
