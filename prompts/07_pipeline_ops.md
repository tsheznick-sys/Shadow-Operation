# 07 PIPELINE OPS (tracker, leak finder, briefs)
STATUSES: SCOUTED > VETTED > PREWARMED > CONTACTED > WARM > AUDIT SENT > CALL BOOKED > TEST RUNNING > COMMITTED > BUILDING > LAUNCHING > PAID > NURTURE / COLD / DECLINED / HOLD.
TRACKER: templates/creator_tracker.csv. Update after every action.
LEAK FINDER (/leaks): input counts per stage: contacted, replied, positive, audit sent, audit viewed, call booked, call held, test run, test GO, committed, executed, paid. Compute stage-to-stage conversion; biggest leak -> ONE fix:
contacted->reply low = targeting/hook/timing. reply->positive low = offer clarity/trust. audit sent->viewed low = delivery friction. viewed->call low = weak audit or ask too big. call->commit low = split/trust/reliability. test->GO low = niche/product fit. commit->executed low = reliability screening. executed->paid low = product/price/traffic.
Only compare variants with 100+ sends each. Course-marketing benchmarks (10-15 replies per 100) are ASSUMPTIONS until your own data replaces them.
DAILY BRIEF (/brief): (1) money in/out today, (2) replies waiting (SLA 1h), (3) tomorrow's P1 sends (max 20-30), (4) launches live + today's checklist, (5) biggest leak, (6) biggest risk, (7) the ONE thing.
WEEKLY (/weekly): funnel table, cash, hours spent, revenue per hour, cost per signed creator, kill/continue decision per creator, per niche, per product. Rank niches by net $ per operator-hour.
KILL RULES: creator silent 7 days post-reply -> COLD. Demand RED -> HOLD. 2 execution failures mid-launch -> pause, call, or exit. Niche with 0 GO after 3 tests -> drop niche.
