# PLAYBOOK DIGEST (distilled from the Growth Operator / Shadow Operator workbooks 1-4 + module)
CORE IDEA: partner with creators (10K-100K) who built audiences but don't monetize; you supply product + launch; revenue share; you stay in the background.
4-STEP METHOD: FIND (IG search "[topic] tips", "email for collabs" bios, similar-accounts chevron) -> APPROACH (value-first message + free gameplan/audit) -> DEVELOP (product, Whop split-pay) -> MONETISE (14-day Stories launch).
3 KILLERS: wrong creators (millions of followers), improvement trap (diagnose, don't "fix"), selling mistake (risk removal, no upfront cost).
NUMBERS GAME: 100 outreach -> claimed 10-15 replies -> 5-8 audit reviews -> 2-4 calls -> 1-2 deals (UNVERIFIED).
POSITIONING: "I help [who] to [outcome] by [method]". Lead with value (free gameplan), not the ask.
AUDIT/GAMEPLAN: audit + hidden transformation, top 3 topics, numbers, product suggestion, launch strategy, 3-slide demand test.
LAUNCH FORMULA: 14 days, 3 phases, 11 buy buttons (see prompts/06). Reported split of sales: ~30-40% in first 24h of cart open.
PSYCHOLOGY: ignored != rejected; first deal hardest for least money; risk removal beats added value.
