Part 5: Launch Strategy 
This is where we use something called a 14-day Instagram Stories sequence. It is a structured series of daily Stories that a creator posts over two weeks, designed to warm up their audience before a product goes live. 
The idea is that instead of just dropping a product on an audience out of nowhere and hoping people buy, you spend the two weeks before the launch slowly building context around the problem the product solves. Not in a way that feels like a countdown or an advertisement. More like the creator is just naturally going deeper on a topic their audience already cares about, through polls, questions, behind the scenes content, free tips and personal stories. So that by the time the product actually launches, the audience has already been thinking about that problem for two weeks, has already gotten something valuable for free and is already in the right headspace to buy.

The sequence does two things simultaneously. It builds anticipation and it builds trust. And the result is that when the creator finally announces the product, it does not feel like a pitch. It feels like the natural next step in a conversation that has already been happening.

Section 12: Understanding The Three Phases 
The 14 days for the 14-day instagram story sequence aren't random. They're organised into 3 specific phases that build on each other to transform strangers into buyers. 
Phase 1: Warm-Up (Days 1-3) 
The goal of the warm-up phase is to build anticipation and prime the audience by getting them thinking about the problem the product solves, without revealing the product itself. 
Phase 2: Value Delivery (Days 4-9) 
The goal of the value delivery phase is to build trust and demonstrate expertise by giving the audience something genuinely useful for free, so that by the time the product is revealed, they already believe it will be worth buying. 
Phase 3: Money Days (Days 10-14) 
The goal of the money days phase is to convert the interest built over the previous two phases into sales, using urgency, social proof and direct promotion to drive as many purchases as possible before the launch closes.
